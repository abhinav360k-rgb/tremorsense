TremorSense measures movement. It can't tell you whether you have a condition. Only a doctor can. Your results never leave this phone.

How TremorSense works

Which test?

Some tremors show up when your hand is relaxed, others when you hold it up. Doctors check both.

Get ready

    After a 3-second countdown, the phone records for 11 seconds. Stay relaxed and don't try to stop any shaking.

      How these numbers were measured. The phone's accelerometer recorded the hand for 11 seconds (the first second is discarded). The signal was filtered to remove motion slower than 1 Hz, and a Fast Fourier Transform found the strongest rhythm between 3 and 12 Hz. Strength is the size of that rhythm in m/s². Phones differ, so compare results from the same phone. This report is a measurement, not a diagnosis.
