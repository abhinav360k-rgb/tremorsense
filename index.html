            How these numbers were measured. Tremor: the phone's accelerometer recorded the hand for 11 seconds (the first second is discarded). Motion slower than 1 Hz was filtered out, and a Fast Fourier Transform found the strongest rhythm between 3 and 12 Hz. Strength is the size of that rhythm in m/s². Tapping: the patient tapped two targets in turn for 15 seconds. Speed is correct alternating taps per second, rhythm variation is how much the gaps between taps differ (coefficient of variation), and speed change compares the first and last 5 seconds. Phones differ, so compare results from the same phone. This report is a measurement, not a diagnosis.
